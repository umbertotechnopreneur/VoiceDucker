PRODUCTION NOTES
The supplied blue-purple background and genuine app screenshots were the references for image_gen built-in compositing.
Main/settings/About reference captures are from the installed 1.0.11.0 local app.
The playback detail is from the user's earlier genuine capture; the crop excludes the superseded header/motto.
No Store trial/license state was invented for the screenshots.
Native generator outputs are retained under sources/generated-native. Export-StoreSizes.ps1 performs final size conversion only.
The original generated outputs were 941 x 1672 (portrait) and 1672 x 941 (hero); final PNGs are resized exports, not additional native-resolution detail.
Creative edits used the built-in image_gen tool. The prompt set is in imagegen-prompts.txt.
Review the final files at full size before submitting. The raw captures and exports remain available for comparison.
