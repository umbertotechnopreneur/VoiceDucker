DESCRIPTION

Keep your soundtrack. Give your voice the spotlight.

You talk. The music turns down. You stop. It comes back. That's VoiceDucker. Spotify, browser videos, and other Windows playback can make room for your voice without another trip to the volume slider.

TALK TO AI. KEEP THE MUSIC.
Chat naturally with an AI assistant while music or videos play in the background. When you speak, playback steps back. Your soundtrack stays. It just learns some manners.

KEEP BROWSER AUDIO IN ITS PLACE.
A tab starts playing while you're talking? Include other apps so browser playback can step back too, instead of drowning you out.

YOUR DEMO, NOT YOUR PLAYLIST.
Use VoiceDucker alongside OBS Studio, Microsoft Clipchamp, or other streaming and recording tools to keep unrelated Windows playback lower while you narrate a demo, tutorial, or stream.

TAKE THE CALL, NOT THE VOLUME CONTROLS.
Keep background music from competing with you during Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and similar meetings. Hide your meeting app if you want to leave its playback untouched.

Put some audio on, pick your microphone, and press Enable. Spotify is included by default. Switch on "Also lower other apps" for other players and browsers. When the microphone picks up sound, their audio gets quieter. When things go quiet, it gently comes back. Opening a browser or joining a call alone does not trigger the volume drop.

VoiceDucker controls Windows playback volume, not the recording software's internal mixer or audio tracks. What reaches a recording or audience depends on your capture source and audio routing. Check a short recording before your demo or stream.

Choose how much the volume drops, how smoothly it fades, and how long it waits through little pauses. The green microphone meter helps you adjust sensitivity.

Your microphone starts off by default. Enable microphone on startup opts into monitoring whenever VoiceDucker opens, including manual launches. Audio stays on your device, in memory: no recordings, transcription, or uploads.

A native Windows 11 app built with WinUI 3, with ongoing attention to RAM, performance, and disk space.
