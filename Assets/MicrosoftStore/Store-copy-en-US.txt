DESCRIPTION

You talk. The music turns down. You stop. It comes back. That's VoiceDucker.

VoiceDucker is a paid app with a 30-day trial. To continue using it after the trial ends, purchase VoiceDucker through Microsoft Store at the price shown in the Store.

Talking to an AI assistant? On a call? Watching a video while music keeps you company? You shouldn't need to grab the volume slider every time you open your mouth. VoiceDucker gives your voice a little room.

Put some audio on, pick your microphone, and press Enable. Spotify is included by default. Switch on "Also lower other apps" for other players and browsers. When the microphone picks up sound, their audio gets quieter. When things go quiet, it gently comes back. Your soundtrack stays. It just learns some manners.

Choose how much the volume drops, how smoothly it fades, and how long it waits through little pauses. The green microphone meter helps you adjust sensitivity.

Your microphone starts off. Audio stays on your device, in memory: no recordings, transcription, or uploads.

A native Windows 11 app built with WinUI 3, with ongoing attention to RAM, performance, and disk space.
