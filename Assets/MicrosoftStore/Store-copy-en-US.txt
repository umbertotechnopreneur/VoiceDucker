DESCRIPTION

You talk. Music turns down. You stop. It comes back.

Keep your soundtrack during AI chats, calls and demos. VoiceDucker lowers selected Windows playback when your microphone detects sound, then brings it back when things go quiet.

Play some music and press Enable. Spotify is included by default. Turn on "Also lower other apps" for browsers and other players. Hide any app you want to leave alone, including your meeting app.

YOUR LISTENING SETUP
Start with five presets: Talking to AI, Client calls, Meetings, Recording a demo and Relaxed conversation. Save personal profiles with your reduction, fade times and detection settings. Switch profiles manually when you need a different setup. Hidden apps stay excluded across all profiles.

YOUR AMOUNT OF QUIET
Choose how far playback drops, how quickly it fades and how long it waits before returning. Softer transitions gently eases the start and end of fades. Each app's slider shows its current volume; Normal shows the level used when detected sound ends. Windows master volume stays unchanged.

TAKE A BREAK
Pause monitoring for 5, 15 or 30 minutes. Resume early or cancel the timer. The microphone stops during the break, and automatic resumption requires successful audio readiness and license checks.

YOUR MICROPHONE, YOUR CHOICE
Choose your microphone and tune detection for your room. Monitoring is off by default. Start with Windows and Enable microphone on startup are independent choices. The microphone startup preference applies on the next launch, including manual launches.

Audio is processed locally in memory, without microphone recordings, speech transcription or audio uploads. Preferences and profiles are saved on your device.

GOOD TO KNOW
VoiceDucker reacts to sound levels, so background noise can also trigger it. Profiles do not enable monitoring or change your microphone device or startup preferences. Disable and exit restore sessions changed by the app to their saved startup safety levels; restoration problems are reported.

For OBS Studio, Microsoft Clipchamp or similar tools, VoiceDucker changes Windows playback. What reaches a recording or stream depends on your audio sources and routing. Check a short recording before your demo or stream.

Built for Windows 11 with WinUI 3.
